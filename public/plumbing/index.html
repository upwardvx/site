What it does each week

  1. Monday numbersAt 7 every Monday: revenue, jobs, callbacks and who owes you, in five lines or fewer, plus one thing worth a look. On a quiet week it says nothing.
  2. Overdue invoicesEvery Tuesday, one reminder drafted per overdue invoice, by email or text. Disputed invoices and anyone reminded in the last week are left out.
  3. Quotes that sitWeekday mornings, one short follow-up for each quote sent 3 to 14 days ago. A quote already accepted gets nothing, and no quote gets a third.
  4. Your team approvesEach draft arrives with the record behind it and three choices: approve, edit or discard. There is no approve-all for anything a customer sees.

Before you see a draft, every dollar figure, invoice and quote number in it is checked against your records. If one doesn’t match, the draft never reaches you.

Where it runs
Inside the tools you already use: your job or invoicing software, your email and texts.
Where you approve
Each draft comes to you with the record behind it and three choices: approve, edit or discard.
What it can see
Only the records the job needs. You can switch off its access any day.
