Off your desk

What it does each week

  1. Missing documentsEvery Tuesday, one email or text per client that names only what’s still missing, never what they already sent. Returns on extension, anyone chased in the last week and anyone chased three times are left out.
  2. Waiting on someone elseA client whose only missing form is late from the issuer, like a brokerage that hasn’t sent its 1099‑Bs, is held, with the reason. So is a client who asks you to estimate a missing form: that’s a preparer’s call, and it comes to you.
  3. Monday statusAt 7 every Monday, five lines or fewer on what’s waiting, stuck and ready, plus one thing worth a look. When waiting on client jumps, it says why.
  4. Last year’s feesOne reminder per overdue invoice, every Tuesday. Disputed invoices are left out, and so are clients whose return is stuck on a late form from someone else.

Each draft arrives with the record behind it and three choices: approve, edit or discard. There is no approve-all for anything a client sees. Every form, tax year and figure is checked against the record first, and if one doesn’t match, you never see the draft.

For a document reminder, the model sees what’s missing, what’s in and the client’s note. It never sees a tax ID or a figure from the return.
